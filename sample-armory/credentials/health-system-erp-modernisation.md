# Regional Health System — Finance & Supply Chain ERP Modernisation

## Situation

An eight-hospital regional health system was running finance and supply chain on
a 19-year-old ERP with 40+ bolt-on integrations. Month-end close took 14 business
days. Supply chain had no reliable item master, so the same clinical item existed
under as many as six part numbers across facilities. A prior implementation
attempt had been paused after nine months with no go-live.

The system had selected a cloud ERP but had no confidence in its systems
integrator's plan and no internal PMO capacity to hold the SI to account.

## What Aberdeen did

Aberdeen was engaged as **implementation assurance and programme PMO** —
explicitly not as a second systems integrator.

- Rebaselined the programme plan and re-scoped Wave 1 from "all modules, all
  facilities" to finance core plus two pilot facilities
- Stood up a programme PMO with weekly delivery cadence, a single RAID log, and
  a steering committee that made decisions rather than receiving updates
- Ran an independent data-readiness assessment that found 61% of supplier
  records were duplicates or inactive, and designed the cleansing approach
- Built the item master rationalisation method and governed its execution across
  the eight facilities
- Designed and ran the cutover control process, including a full mock cutover
  six weeks before go-live
- Managed hypercare for 90 days post-go-live with a defect triage protocol

## Outcome

- Go-live delivered on the rebaselined date with no Sev-1 defects in the first
  30 days
- Month-end close reduced from 14 business days to 6
- Supplier master reduced from 47,000 records to 18,400 active, validated records
- Item master duplication reduced by 71% across facilities
- Programme delivered 4% under the rebaselined budget
- Client retained Aberdeen for Wave 2 (remaining six facilities) without a
  competitive re-bid

## Why this is relevant to ERP pursuits

This is the reference for any RFP asking for ERP implementation assurance, data
migration governance, or recovery of a paused programme. The distinguishing
detail is the SI-independence stance — Aberdeen held the integrator to account
rather than absorbing its scope.
