# Regional Bank — Core Platform Programme Recovery & PMO

## Situation

A regional bank was 16 months into a core platform modernisation that had gone
red. Three of four workstreams were behind schedule, the vendor and the bank
disagreed on scope in writing, and the board had asked for an independent view
before approving further funding. A concurrent regulatory remediation commitment
shared the same delivery team, so slippage on one created exposure on the other.

## What Aberdeen did

- Ran a two-week independent programme assessment and presented findings to the
  board audit committee, including the parts the client did not want to hear
- Rebuilt the integrated programme plan with the vendor in the room, producing a
  single plan both parties signed
- Separated the regulatory remediation workstream onto its own governance track
  with a dedicated lead, removing the resource contention
- Stood up a recovery PMO: weekly delivery cadence, escalation SLAs, a decision
  log with named owners, and a status format the board could actually read
- Renegotiated the vendor statement of work to tie payment milestones to
  demonstrated functionality rather than elapsed time
- Coached the bank's programme director through the recovery rather than
  replacing them — capability transfer was an explicit deliverable

## Outcome

- Programme moved from red to amber in 10 weeks, to green in 19
- Regulatory remediation commitment met on its original date
- Vendor SOW restructured, moving roughly $2.1M of payment from time-based to
  functionality-based milestones
- Board approved the remaining funding tranche after the Aberdeen readout
- Bank's own programme director retained the role post-recovery

## Why this is relevant to BFS and PMO pursuits

Use this for any RFP involving programme recovery, PMO stand-up, independent
assessment, or regulatory delivery under time pressure. The strongest detail for
an evaluator is that Aberdeen presented unwelcome findings to a board and still
retained the relationship — that is what independence looks like in practice.
