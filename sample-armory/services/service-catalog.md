# Aberdeen Advisors — Service Catalogue

Aberdeen only proposes from this menu. If an RFP asks for something outside it,
that is a teaming or a no-bid conversation, not a stretch claim.

## Healthcare

- **Revenue cycle transformation** — charge capture, denials management, patient
  access redesign, and the operating model to sustain it.
- **Clinical operations improvement** — throughput, capacity planning, care model
  redesign across acute and ambulatory settings.
- **Payer–provider integration** — contract operations, value-based care
  enablement, prior-authorisation workflow redesign.
- **Health system ERP and EHR adjacency** — finance and supply chain
  modernisation alongside a clinical platform, without touching the EHR core.
- **Regulatory readiness** — CMS interoperability, price transparency, HIPAA
  operational controls.

## Banking & Financial Services (BFS)

- **Core banking and payments modernisation** — target-state design, vendor
  selection, phased migration.
- **Risk and regulatory programme delivery** — BSA/AML, CECL, and remediation
  programme management.
- **Wealth and advisory platform enablement** — advisor desktop, onboarding,
  and client-servicing workflow.
- **Post-merger integration** — Day 1 readiness, systems rationalisation,
  operating model convergence.

## ERP

- **ERP strategy and vendor selection** — requirements, RFP management,
  scoring, negotiation support.
- **ERP implementation assurance** — independent oversight of a systems
  integrator, not a second SI.
- **Data migration and reconciliation** — cleansing, mapping, cutover control,
  and post-go-live reconciliation.
- **Post-go-live stabilisation** — hypercare, defect triage, adoption recovery.

## PMO & Programme Delivery

- **PMO stand-up** — governance, cadence, reporting, and the decision rights
  that make it stick.
- **Programme recovery** — for programmes that are red and need to be green.
- **Portfolio prioritisation** — capacity-based sequencing against strategy.
- **Benefits realisation** — baselining, tracking, and reporting outcomes.

## Organisational Change

- **Change strategy and impact assessment**
- **Stakeholder engagement and communications**
- **Training design and delivery**
- **Adoption measurement and course correction**

## Managed Services

- **Application managed services** — run and enhance after go-live.
- **Process managed services** — finance, HR, and revenue-cycle operations.
- **Transition and knowledge capture** — the part most providers do badly.
- **Continuous improvement** — a committed year-on-year efficiency target.

## AI & Data Enablement

- **AI readiness assessment and roadmap**
- **Document intelligence** — extraction, classification, summarisation
- **Internal copilots and knowledge retrieval**
- **Workflow and reporting automation**
- **Data governance and readiness**

## Delivery models

| Model | When it fits |
|---|---|
| Fixed-fee milestone | Well-defined scope with a firm deliverable set |
| Time & materials with a cap | Discovery, assessment, and advisory work |
| Managed service (monthly) | Ongoing run and enhance, multi-year |
| Outcome-linked | Where a baseline is measurable and the client will share risk |
