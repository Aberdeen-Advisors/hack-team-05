# Aberdeen Engagement Model

Three phases. Proposals should map the client's ask onto these rather than
inventing a bespoke methodology for every pursuit.

## Phase 1 — Understand & Prioritise (4–8 weeks)

Establish the baseline and agree what matters.

- Current-state assessment against a defined operating-model framework
- Stakeholder interviews and process observation
- Data collection and quantified baseline
- Opportunity identification, sized and sequenced
- Prioritised roadmap with a business case

**Typical team:** Engagement Partner (10%), Engagement Manager (50%), two
Consultants (100%), SME as needed.

**Deliverables:** current-state assessment, quantified opportunity register,
prioritised roadmap, business case, executive readout.

## Phase 2 — Prove & Validate (8–16 weeks)

Prove the top opportunities work here, not in theory.

- Pilot design with explicit success criteria agreed up front
- Build or configure against the target state
- Controlled rollout to a defined population
- Measurement against the baseline from Phase 1
- Scale-or-stop decision, taken on evidence

**Typical team:** Engagement Manager (50%), Solution Lead (100%), two to four
Consultants/Analysts (100%), Change Lead (50%).

**Deliverables:** pilot design, working solution, measured results, scale
recommendation with a go/no-go.

## Phase 3 — Scale & Operate (16+ weeks, or managed service)

Move from a proven pilot to business as usual.

- Enterprise rollout by wave
- Operating model and role design
- Capability transfer to client staff — this is a deliverable, not a courtesy
- Benefits tracking against the Phase 1 baseline
- Transition to managed service or to client run

**Typical team:** Engagement Manager (30%), Delivery Lead (100%), Consultants by
wave, Change and Training Leads.

**Deliverables:** rollout plan, operating model, training and capability
transfer, benefits report, run-book.

## Governance that runs across all phases

- Weekly delivery stand-up with the client counterpart
- Bi-weekly steering committee with a written status and a decision log
- A single RAID log, owned by Aberdeen, visible to the client
- Monthly benefits review once a baseline exists
- Named escalation path to the Engagement Partner, with a 24-hour response
  commitment

## What Aberdeen will not do

- Take a fixed-fee implementation without a completed discovery phase
- Act as both the systems integrator and the assurance provider on the same
  programme
- Commit to a benefits number without an agreed measurement baseline
