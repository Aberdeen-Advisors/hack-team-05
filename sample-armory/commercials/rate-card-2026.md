## Managed services

Priced monthly against a defined service scope, not hourly.

| Tier | Monthly range | Includes |
|---|---|---|
| Foundation | $45k–$75k | Monitoring, L2 support, standard SLA |
| Standard | $75k–$140k | L2/L3 support, enhancement pool, continuous improvement target |
| Enterprise | $140k–$280k+ | Full run, dedicated team, committed efficiency target |

## Discount guardrails

- **0–5%** — routine, no approval needed
- **5–10%** — Engagement Partner approval; requires volume or multi-phase commitment
- **10–15%** — Practice Lead approval; strategic logo or multi-year only
- **Above 15%** — requires a written commercial rationale and firm leadership
  approval. Rare, and usually a signal the scope should shrink instead

Prefer reducing scope to reducing rate. A discounted rate resets the client's
expectation permanently; a smaller Phase 1 does not.

## Commercial structures

| Structure | When to propose | Watch out for |
|---|---|---|
| Fixed fee, milestone-based | Scope is genuinely defined | Change control must be explicit |
| T&M with not-to-exceed | Discovery and assessment | Cap must be believable, not aspirational |
| Monthly managed service | Ongoing run, 2+ years | Volume assumptions need a reset clause |
| Outcome-linked | Measurable baseline exists and client will share risk | Never without an agreed baseline |

## Expenses

Billed at cost, capped at 8% of professional fees unless the RFP states otherwise.
Where an RFP requires expenses included in rates, price the loading explicitly
rather than absorbing it silently.
