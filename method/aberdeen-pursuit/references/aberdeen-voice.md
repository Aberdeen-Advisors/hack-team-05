# Aberdeen Voice and Production Standards

Sources: Client Response & Deliverable Playbook v1.0 Section 8; Aberdeen Foundational
Strategy v1.0 (Brand Tone Notes, Value Proposition).

---

## Voice

Professional, credible, relationship-driven, value-focused. Avoids jargon while signaling
consulting sophistication. Plain, confident, concise.

The firm's own framing, worth echoing where it fits naturally:

- "We partner with executives to turn strategy into measurable outcomes."
- "We focus on the moments where execution matters most."
- "We don't recommend what we wouldn't implement ourselves."
- "We stay accountable through delivery, not just direction."
- Outcomes, "not slideware."
- Low-ego, high-ownership. Senior-led.

Use these as tone calibration, not as text to paste into every document. A proposal that
quotes the value proposition three times reads like a brochure.

---

## Writing rules

**Hard rules:**

- **No em dashes.** Use hyphens, commas, or a full stop. This is an explicit brand
  standard and the most common tell.
- Vary sentence length. Uniform medium-length sentences read as generated.
- No filler. Cut "it is important to note," "in today's rapidly evolving landscape,"
  "leverage" as a verb where "use" works.
- Active voice, named actors. "Aberdeen delivers X" beats "X will be delivered."
- Specific over superlative. "Reduced non-billable prep from 30 hours to 4" beats
  "dramatically improves efficiency."

**AI tells to scan for and remove** (Gate 5 checks these literally):

| Tell | Replace with |
|---|---|
| Em dash, the character shown here between backticks `—` (this row is the specimen to scan for, not a violation) | hyphen, comma, or full stop |
| "delve into" | "examine", "look at" |
| "robust", "seamless", "cutting-edge", "best-in-class" | the specific property you mean |
| "It's not just X, it's Y" | say what it is |
| "In today's fast-paced..." | delete |
| "landscape", "realm", "tapestry" | the actual noun |
| Tricolon everywhere ("clarity, discipline, and rigor") | vary the rhythm |
| "Furthermore", "Moreover", "Additionally" stacked | join or cut |
| Section openers that restate the heading | start with the content |
| Uniform bullet lengths across a whole document | let them differ |

## Brand standards

| Element | Standard |
|---|---|
| Colors | Aberdeen Blue `#09375F`, Verdigris `#44B0B1`, Onyx `#404040`, White |
| Chart secondary | Deep Sky Blue `#5CC8FF`, Jade `#00A676`, Gold `#F7D002`, Jasper `#DB504A` |
| Typography | Poppins for headings and display; Arial or Poppins for body |
| Logo | Primary on white/light, white on blue/dark. Keep clear space. Never stretch or recolor. |
| Figures | Branded diagrams over stock art. Number and caption every figure. Export at 2x. |
| Tables | Blue header row, zebra striping, thin lines, left-aligned text, scannable rows |
| Accessibility | ADA contrast. Caption video. Use real heading structure, not bold text. |
| File naming | `Client_Document_Type_Date`, e.g. `Acme_Response_AI_RFP`. Use the real client name in the actual file, never in this repository. |

**Do not hand-roll formatting.** Invoke the `aberdeen-brand` skill to produce the .docx,
.pptx, or .xlsx. It is the single source of truth for brand application, and using it is
what keeps output consistent across pursuits and across people.

Version deliberately. Keep an editable source and a locked PDF.

## Structural habits that read as senior

- Lead a section with the client's situation, then Aberdeen's response to it.
- Put the number in the sentence, not in a footnote.
- Name the person, not the role, when a person is accountable.
- State what is out of scope as confidently as what is in scope.
- Where something is genuinely unknown, say so and say what would resolve it. Confident
  hedging beats false precision, and evaluators notice.
