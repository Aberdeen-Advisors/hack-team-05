# Mock RFPs

Fictional sample RFPs used to test the proposal generator. Every company here is
invented for internal testing — none is a real business, and no document contains
confidential material.

## Documents

- **[cascadia-outdoor-brands-rfp.pdf](cascadia-outdoor-brands-rfp.pdf)** — Cascadia
  Outdoor Brands, a four-brand outdoor apparel house seeking IT/ERP modernization;
  five deliverables (D1–D5), evaluation criteria weighted 25/25/20/15/15, and a
  non-negotiable constraint of no material permanent growth in IT headcount.
- **[sonora-iced-tea-rfp.pdf](sonora-iced-tea-rfp.pdf)** — Sonora Iced Tea, which has
  held a 99c price point since 1993, seeking a five-year cost and margin strategy;
  non-negotiable constraint of no wage or benefit cuts and no recipe downgrades, with
  25% of the score on "Understanding of Brand Promise & Culture".
- **[wayfarer-market-rfp.pdf](wayfarer-market-rfp.pdf)** — Wayfarer Market Co., a
  specialty grocer with roughly 500 stores and about 50,000 "crew", seeking revenue
  growth without eroding crew culture; 30% of the score on "Understanding of Brand &
  Crew Culture", and it explicitly rejects the generic cut-costs / loyalty-program /
  more-ads playbook.

## Shared structure

All three follow the same section layout, so they can be parsed the same way:
Scope of Work, Required Deliverables (D1–D5), Proposal Requirements (5.1–5.7),
weighted Evaluation Criteria, timeline, page limit, and a single non-negotiable
constraint.
